## Background

Article thesis surfaced during [SUG-166](https://linear.app/sugartown/issue/SUG-166/glossary-completion-gap-fill-eds-vocabulary-import) glossary work (Headless CMS extended definition).

**Core argument:** The real trade-off in headless/composable (MACH) is maximum front-end flexibility vs the perceived WYSIWYG editorial access of traditional platforms. Historically this kept headless in larger orgs with engineering resources. That perception is changing as the composable space matures: yes, there's more up-front front-end load to design a MACH system, but the editorial experience and flexibility can be deliberately built in for the long haul, rather than inherited from a template.

Good fit for Bex voice + the platform/CMS positioning. Pairs with the /platform/cms surface and the glossary terms Headless CMS, Composable architecture, Content-as-code, Structured content.
