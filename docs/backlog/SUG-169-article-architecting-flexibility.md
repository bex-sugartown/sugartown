## Background

Article thesis surfaced during [SUG-166](https://linear.app/sugartown/issue/SUG-166/glossary-completion-gap-fill-eds-vocabulary-import) glossary work (Flexibility extended definition).

**Core argument:** Flexibility is often where stakeholder happiness is won or lost. Between the design system and the content model, you can deliberately architect a certain amount of give — enough that business users *feel* they have the flexibility they need and don't feel stymied by a DS or content model, without actually surrendering consistency. The craft is balancing real constraint with enough perceived freedom that people stop fighting the guardrails.

Pairs with the headless/composable trade-offs article ([SUG-168](https://linear.app/sugartown/issue/SUG-168/article-headlesscomposable-cms-trade-offs-front-end-load-vs-built-in)) and the glossary terms Flexibility, Reusability, Contribution Model, Content model.
