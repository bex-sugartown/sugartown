3. Behaviour annotations (nav surface, all six)

ItemSpec
Active stateActive slide is the one at least 60% in view (existing IntersectionObserver, threshold 0.6). Active dot: filled --st-color-pink, scale 1.25. Inactive dot: 2px ring, transparent fill (was a 15% white fill, invisible). Ring colour is theme-aware: --st-color-white-85 in dark, a dark neutral in light (activation audit: pick the existing border or text-secondary token that gives at least 3:1 on the light surface). The arrows and caption bar stay dark in both themes because they sit on the image, not the page.
Hash/anchor behaviourNone. Slides do not write to the URL.
Scroll-spyYes: manual scroll, arrows and dots all update the active dot via the observer.

Active slide click: follows the link as configured in Studio (openInNewTab respected). Arrow: wraps at the ends (existing). Keyboard: Left/Right arrow on the track moves one slide (new).

4. Contrast, measured

Caption text on the caption bar, worst case being a white pixel under the bar (255 background, scrim over it). Computed as composite of black at alpha over white.

Figures are hand-computed for this vspec and are to be re-measured with pnpm validate:contrast at implementation (the Evidence cell for the AC). Arrow glyph: white on black 90% with a 2px white ring, 3:1 non-text contrast against any image because the ring is white and the fill is near black on both sides.

5. Image section treatments and legibility

Treatment (treatment prop to Media overlay)Rule for legibility
noneCaption bar uses the 90% scrim. Nothing else changes.
duotone / scrim overlayOverlay stays at z-index 2, caption bar at z-index 3, so caption always sits above the treatment. The bar is opaque enough that the treatment colour never reaches the text.
Caption lengthClamp stays at 2 lines in the carousel; the full text is in the figure's accessible name. Grid and masonry use the same bar.
CreditNeutral white 85%, never seafoam: seafoam read as a link on the live page.
Link affordanceActive slide only. Cursor is a pointer over the image when linked. No new badge in this epic (open decision 2).

7. Decisions (answered 2026-11-06)

  1. Caption style: overlay at 90% black.
  2. Link affordance on the active slide: none, pointer cursor only.
  3. Arrow position: centred on the image (needs a wrapper around the track so heading and dots are excluded).
