# Why My Portfolio Has a Changelog

It's worth noting that most portfolio sites are frozen the day they launch. Mine ships a version number in the footer — v0.28.5 at the time of writing — and a changelog that anyone can read.

The reason is simple: the site is the portfolio. Not the case studies on it, the site itself. Every release, every migration, every post-mortem is a work sample. I utilize the same release discipline here that I'd demand on a client project, because a portfolio that claims process maturity while being deployed by vibes is lying about the one thing it exists to prove.

The changelog also keeps me honest about pace — a portfolio that hasn't shipped anything in eight months is making a claim too, just not the one its owner intended.

Version numbers on a personal site look like an affectation until you need to answer "what changed and when." Then they look like the only sane choice.
